Ironstack development build - Linux x64

Built on Ubuntu 24.04 x64; older glibc distributions are not validated.
Contents: compiled VST3 bundle and Standalone, provenance, checksums and notices.
M3 adds centered octave-down bass, a clean sine and oversampled upper drive.
Controls: Output/Split/Destruction (double-click numerical entry) and Panic.
Guitar uses eight stereo unison voices. Latency: 32 samples. Guitar drive/filters,
final modes/UI and FX remain future
work; the high-note max-drive alias target remains open for M5.

Extract the tar.gz preserving executable permissions. Copy the entire
Ironstack.vst3 folder to ~/.vst3 and rescan your host. Run ./Ironstack for the
Standalone and choose audio/MIDI devices in its settings. An X11 display or
compatible XWayland session and ALSA are required. System runtime libraries
(glibc/libstdc++, X11, ALSA, FreeType and fontconfig) are not bundled; see
ci-environment.txt for the exact build environment. No JACK/browser is required.
You may replace the LGPL system libraries and reverse-engineer this program
as needed to debug modifications to those libraries. These instructions do not
restrict those LGPL rights; the libraries remain separate and replaceable.

CI verifies build/tests under Xvfb, not physical audio devices or DAW playback.
No Linux DAW/listening acceptance is claimed. No runtime networking is used.
