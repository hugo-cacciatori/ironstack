Ironstack development build - macOS Apple silicon (arm64)

Built on macOS 15 for Apple silicon with deployment target macOS 12.0.
Older systems and Intel Macs are not validated. The package contains a compiled
VST3 bundle and Ironstack.app, provenance, checksums and third-party notices.
M3 adds centered octave-down bass, a clean sine and oversampled upper drive.
Controls: Output/Split/Destruction (double-click numerical entry) and Panic.
Guitar uses eight stereo unison voices. Latency: 32 samples. Guitar drive/filters,
final modes/UI and FX remain future
work; the high-note max-drive alias target remains open for M5.

Extract the tar.gz preserving app/bundle structure and executable permissions.
Copy the entire Ironstack.vst3 bundle to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and rescan
your VST3 host. Copy Ironstack.app to a folder of your choice for Standalone.
Choose audio and MIDI devices in its settings. No runtime networking is used.

Development build only: no Developer ID certificate/notarization is provided or
purchased. macOS may block downloaded apps/plugins. Review provenance/checksums;
use macOS Privacy & Security controls only if you trust this build. Do not disable
system security globally. A paid Apple developer account is not required to build.
